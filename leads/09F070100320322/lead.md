1. Does the C-2 district text (zoning ordinance, Chapter 80; the C-2 use list is cited as Sec. 80-84(c)) allow warehouse or distribution use, by right or by use permit? The code is on Municode, which blocks plain fetches; it takes minutes in a browser.
2. Which category of the 2025 comprehensive plan covers this parcel: Highway Mixed-Use, Light Industrial, Industrial or another? Ask Fairburn Planning & Zoning or read the plan's map pages by eye.
3. Does the 2025 plan's call to limit development in the SR 74/Senoia Road corridor apply here, and has the city acted on it (moratorium, corridor study)?
4. Would the city support a rezoning from C-2 to M-1? Under the 2021 plan, Office/Industrial goes with O&I, M-1 and M-2.
5. Is the parcel inside the GA Highway 74 overlay district, and were conditions attached to the C-2 zoning? Ask for a zoning verification letter.
6. Where are the nearest water main and sewer line, what sizes, and is there capacity for about 150,000 SF? Only Fairburn Utilities can say.
7. What does the Secretary of State record show for the owner (status, date formed, registered agent, general partner, who can sign)? The site blocks plain fetches; look it up by hand.
8. Is there a broker sign on site, a recorded option, or a legal notice? Is the owner's adjoining land (67.9 ac together per the brief) being marketed as one piece?
9. Ask the city for closed rezoning cases on Senoia Road near I-85 in the last five years; only open cases were checked.
