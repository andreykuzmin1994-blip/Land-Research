## Post-change review (round 1)

An independent adversarial review in fresh contexts (correctness and
integrity) re-ran the corridor import on its own database and probed the
code; a skeptic pass then checked each finding. Twelve findings were
confirmed (one high, eight should-fix, three nits). Every one is fixed with a
test; none is deferred. Residual risk is recorded in SR-16 (finding 2).
