# Is it really land? Built check of the top 250 (run fulton-2026-11-13-screens)

Checked 2026-11-16. Three signals for each of the 250 highest-ranked parcels:

1. **Building footprints** (FEMA USA Structures) inside the parcel's *outline*, with holes in the county parcel shape filled in. 6715 Oakley Industrial Blvd (#16) slipped through the screen because its building sits in such a hole, on a separate improvements-only tax record.
2. **Other tax records inside the outline** (top 300 by rank), with their improvement values.
3. **The county's 2024 aerial photo**, judged by a reviewer that saw only the photo. Where the photo and the footprints disagreed, or the reviewer was unsure (71 parcels), a second reviewer looked again with both in hand. The 2024 photo wins over the older footprints.

**Result:** 112 raw land, 80 partly usable, 54 not raw land, 4 unclear (#250's photo would not export).

The screen removes parcels whose own tax record shows buildings. It cannot see buildings on a separate record, paved truck and trailer yards, outdoor storage, quarries, landfills or house sites with low improvement values. That is most of what this check found.
