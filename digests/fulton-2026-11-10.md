## Read the A list with care

A means every one of the screen's own checks came back clear. It does not
know who will sell, and the pilot above shows it does not know who has
already bought or entitled. Several other A owners look like operating
companies or investors with their own plans (a quarry operator, a
warehouse club, a net-lease owner, a railroad's property arm). That is a
reading of the names, not a checked fact.
