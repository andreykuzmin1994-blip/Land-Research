**What this says about the screen** (for the operator; nothing changed here):

1. **The "land-only" screen lets built sites through.** `screens.py`
   rejects a parcel only when its improvements are both over the cap and
   over half the land value. #4 ($679k on $2.77M of land), #29 (a
   terminal) and #145 ($951k) all passed. Changing this is the
   operator's decision (`screens.py` is human-owned and needs a new
   baseline).
2. **Atlanta's future land use was read from a superseded field.**
   `fulton.py` read `LANDUSEDES`. The city's current layer is drawn from
   `NDP`, the development pattern of the plan passed in July 2025. This
   alone made #156 and #195 grade A. A fix is proposed on
   `claude/magical-dirac-0kll8l`. It does not touch the evaluation, where
   future land use is blank.
3. **A listed hazardous site next door does not count.** #13 and #145
   border the same Class IV cleanup site, and their briefs say nothing.
4. **Owner-users and their buffer land rank as leads.** These are a
   quarry (#43, #50), a waste hauler (#48), a retailer's distribution
   site (#174) and a railroad (#2). The owner-type screens do not catch
   operating companies holding land next to their own operations.
